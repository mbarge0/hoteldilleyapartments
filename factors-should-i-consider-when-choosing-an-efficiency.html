What should I check inside the unit before booking?

For a month-long work trip, start with the features that will affect the stay every day. At Hotel Dilley Grand, the standard rooms are furnished studio-style units of approximately 210 square feet. Each standard room has a queen bed, private tile bathroom with a shower and tub, television, mini fridge, desk, and dresser. Rooms are rented move-in ready and fully furnished, so furniture does not need to be brought to the property.

Sleeping capacity is another practical point. Standard rooms accommodate up to three guests, while the separate apartment unit accommodates up to four. A person traveling alone may have different space needs from a crew manager arranging accommodations for several workers. Confirm which type of unit is being considered and whether its stated capacity fits the group.

Food preparation should be checked directly. Some units include kitchenettes, but not every room does. A mini fridge is included in the rooms, while a kitchenette depends on the unit. Anyone who expects to prepare meals during the month should ask whether the available unit includes a kitchenette rather than assuming that all efficiency units have the same setup.

For work and downtime, rooms include a desk and cable television. WiFi is available throughout the property. These listed features can be compared with the needs of the assignment, including whether the guest expects to complete computer work from the room.

The building layout may also matter. Rooms 101 through 109 are on the first floor, and rooms 201 through 210 are on the second floor. There is also one separate apartment unit. Ask which unit type and floor are available if those details affect the decision.

Hotel Dilley Grand is a simple, practical, no-frills property rather than a resort or full-service hotel. The main question is whether the furnished room, bathroom, desk, storage, connectivity, capacity, and available cooking setup match the actual needs of a month-long assignment.

How do location and rental terms affect a month-long stay?

For a month-long work assignment, consider both the address and the type of rental arrangement. Hotel Dilley Grand is at 110 S Main Street, Dilley, Texas 78017, in Frio County. The property sits on South Main Street close to Interstate 35, between San Antonio and Laredo. Compare that location with the work site and the travel route required during the assignment before choosing a unit.

The property is permitted under its certificate of occupancy to offer both nightly stays and longer-term leases in the same building. That gives a worker or crew manager two possible ways to structure a stay without changing properties. A guest can begin with a short trip and move to a longer arrangement without changing address. The right arrangement depends on how firm the work schedule is and whether the assignment may be extended.

Rates and availability should be requested through an appropriate booking channel or directly from the property.

It is also useful to understand the type of operation. Hotel Dilley Grand is an independently owned and operated 20-unit hotel rather than part of a national chain. An on-site manager lives at the property. The hotel was purchased in June 2024, renovated, and reopened on July 24, 2024. The property is intended for short trips, temporary work assignments, contractors and crews, relocations, and monthly stays.
